# Branch Manager Operations

## Module Purpose

Control one or more assigned branches, supervise queues and exceptions, and preserve the separation between operational approval, clinical judgement and ERPNext accounting.

## Daily Branch Control

1. Open Veterinary Home and confirm the selected branch and date scope.
2. Review appointments, waiting and checked-in patients, active consultations, laboratory work, vaccination due work, active hospitalisations, pending dispensary work and billing exceptions visible to the role.
3. Drill from every exception to its source record; assign a named responsible person and due time.
4. Confirm Front Desk registration and appointment queues are progressing without duplicate patients.
5. Confirm clinical decisions remain with the authorised practitioner.
6. Review Billing Center for open sessions and outstanding balances; send invoice or allocation work to Accounts/Cashier.
7. Review Stock Expiry Monitor, shortages, pending issues and warehouse exceptions with Dispensary/Stock.
8. Review Hospitalisation occupancy, pending actions, charges and discharge blockers.

## Weekly Review

- Compare Branch Performance with source reports using the same period and branch.
- Review practitioner, lab, vaccination, grooming, boarding and dispensary performance.
- Review purchase orders due, supplier delays, inventory expiry and receivables ageing.
- Review regulatory setup readiness and report runs awaiting submission or regulator outcome.
- Review access assignments, leavers and users operating outside their assigned branch.

## Controls

- Do not alter diagnoses or treatment to improve a dashboard.
- Do not mark invoices paid or edit submitted ERPNext transactions.
- Do not widen a user's role to clear an operational backlog.
- Do not approve a stock reconciliation without count evidence and variance explanation.

## Practice Exercise

Reconcile one Veterinary Home card to its source rows, assign three branch exceptions to the correct roles, and prepare a daily branch close note with record IDs, owners and due times.
