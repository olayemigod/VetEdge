# Veterinary Home and EdgeSuite Daily Start

## Module Purpose

Use the role-aware Veterinary Home to identify priority work, enter the correct authorised workspace, and preserve branch and access controls across Light and Dark appearance.

## Operating Standard

Veterinary Home is an action centre and operational snapshot. It does not create invoices, appointments, consultations, stock entries or clinical records by itself. Cards, quick actions and drill-downs are permission-aware; a card may be absent when the logged-in role cannot read its source.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Sign in with the named account assigned to your job.
2. Open **Veterinary Home** at `/desk/vetedge`.
3. Confirm the displayed user, company and branch context before using any total or action.
4. Read the attention cards and operational snapshot. Depending on role, these may include appointments, waiting or checked-in patients, active consultations, completed work, laboratory results to review, missed follow-up, pending dispensary work and outstanding invoices.
5. Open a card and confirm its drill-down rows reconcile to the same branch and date scope.
6. Open the authoritative source record or workspace before taking action. Do not correct a transaction from a dashboard total.
7. Use the direct Veterinary sidebar for Veterinary work. Use the Product Menu only to move to another product already permitted to your account.
8. Refresh after completing the source workflow and confirm the attention item or count changes as expected.

## EdgeSuite Appearance and Navigation

- Veterinary Home is a direct sidebar item and has no expand control.
- The first collapsible groups are Dashboard, Clinical Operations and Appointments.
- Light and Dark appearance change presentation only. They do not change roles, branches, filters, records or workflow rules.
- On narrow screens, confirm filters, Link-field selectors, statuses and action buttons remain visible before saving.
- Portal Branding is separately governed in Veterinary Settings and does not control the internal EdgeSuite theme.

## Practice Exercise

1. Open Veterinary Home in the role assigned by the trainer.
2. State the active company and branch.
3. Open one visible attention card and identify the source DocType or page.
4. Return to Home without editing the source.
5. Change appearance, reopen the same card and confirm the record and workflow are unchanged.

## Completion Check

- [ ] Correct role and branch confirmed.
- [ ] Home card reconciled to its drill-down.
- [ ] Source record opened before action.
- [ ] Appearance change did not alter authority.
