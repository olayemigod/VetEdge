# Veterinary Doctor Training Glossary

Use this glossary during doctor training so clinical, front desk, accounts, lab, and dispensary teams use the same words.

| Term | Meaning in VetEdge training |
|---|---|
| Patient | The animal receiving veterinary care. In VetEdge this is the Veterinary Patient record. |
| Pet Owner | The person or customer responsible for the patient. Owner and billing details may link to ERPNext customer records. |
| Master Record | An approved setup value used in forms, such as Consultation Type, Lab Test, Vaccine, Diagnosis, Species, Breed, Treatment Item, Grooming Service, or Kennel. |
| Consultation | The doctor's clinical visit record. It captures complaint, history, assessment, diagnosis, treatment plan, follow-up, and related actions. |
| Consultation Type | A category for the consultation, such as routine visit, emergency, review, or another clinic-defined type. |
| Service Item | An item used to represent a billable or stock-related service. Doctors may see these values, but Accounts, Pharmacy, Dispensary, or Admin should manage setup and corrections. |
| Treatment Plan | The doctor's written clinical plan for medication, procedures, monitoring, follow-up, or referral. |
| Planned Treatment | A structured treatment row that helps the clinic hand off work to nursing, dispensary, billing, or follow-up workflows. |
| Lab Order | A request for diagnostic tests. The doctor requests tests, lab staff process samples and enter results, and the doctor reviews the results. |
| Vaccination | A clinical record showing a vaccine that is planned, awaiting payment, pending administration, administered, or cancelled. |
| Preventive Care | Routine care intended to prevent disease, such as vaccinations, boosters, parasite prevention, and wellness reviews. |
| Hospitalisation | Inpatient care for a patient admitted for observation, treatment, nursing, monitoring, or procedures. |
| Care Location | The kennel, ward, cage, room, or other configured location where an admitted patient is placed. |
| Grooming | A non-clinical service workflow for grooming appointments or sessions. Doctors usually get involved only when medical safety or follow-up is needed. |
| Boarding | A non-clinical service workflow for short-term pet accommodation and routine care. It is different from Hospitalisation. |
| Medical Clearance | A doctor's note or decision that a patient is medically fit for a service such as grooming or boarding, where the clinic uses that step. |
| Handoff | The point where responsibility moves from one role to another, such as Doctor to Front Desk, Accounts, Grooming Staff, Boarding Staff, Nurse, Lab, or Dispensary. |
| Kennel | A configured boarding location used by boarding staff for pet accommodation. |
| Medical Alert | A clinical warning or concern that should be reviewed before care or service continues, such as allergy, aggression, wound, infection, seizure history, or medication need. |
| Owner Instruction | A note from or for the Pet Owner, such as feeding, medication, handling, pickup, or follow-up instructions. Clinical instructions should still be recorded in the appropriate clinical record. |
| Daily Charge | A hospitalisation charge that may be generated for each day or period of inpatient care, based on clinic settings. |
| Payment Gate | A payment rule that may warn or block the next workflow step until payment conditions are met. |
| Submitted Invoice | A Sales Invoice that has been submitted in ERPNext. Submitted invoices are protected for accounting accuracy and should not be manually altered by doctors. |
| Draft Invoice | A Sales Invoice that has been created but not submitted. Accounts or authorised users may still review it before submission. |
| Notification | A message or task alert sent to a user, such as a lab result review, payment warning, missed appointment, vaccination reminder, or hospitalisation action. |
| Follow-up Appointment | A future appointment created so the patient can return for review, repeat treatment, vaccination, lab follow-up, or discharge review. |
| Discharge Readiness | The check that confirms whether a hospitalised patient can be safely discharged, including clinical notes, billing, payment, stock, and pending activity checks. |
| Branch | The operational clinic location handling the work. Branch access may affect what records a doctor can open. |
| Cost Center | The accounting dimension mapped from Branch for ERPNext billing. Doctors should not change accounting allocation manually. |
| Front Desk Handoff | The point where registration, appointment coordination, owner contact, or scheduling moves to Front Desk. |
| Accounts Handoff | The point where invoice submission, payment collection, or invoice correction moves to Accounts or Cashier. |
| Lab Handoff | The point where sample collection, test processing, or result entry moves to Lab Technician or lab staff. |
| Dispensary Handoff | The point where medication picking, stock issue, or stock correction moves to Pharmacy or Dispensary. |
