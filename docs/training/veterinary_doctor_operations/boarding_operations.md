# Boarding Operations

## Module Purpose

Coordinate boarding bookings, kennel availability, care records, owner instructions and medical escalation under branch and capacity controls.

## Booking and Admission Procedure

1. Open the Boarding workflow and confirm Service Branch.
2. Search for the existing patient and owner before creating any new registration.
3. Review booking dates, kennel requirement, vaccination information, medication instructions and owner contact details.
4. Confirm Kennel Availability before accepting the stay.
5. Record the approved booking and required deposit or billing handoff.
6. At check-in, confirm patient identity, belongings, diet, medication, alerts and emergency contact.
7. Record care activities in the Boarding Care Record according to site procedure.
8. At check-out, confirm care completion, owner handover and authorised billing status.

## Clinical Escalation

If the animal becomes ill, injured or clinically unstable, notify the practitioner and create or link the authorised Consultation or Hospitalisation workflow. Boarding notes do not replace clinical records. Linked vaccination history should be reviewed from the patient record; do not mark a vaccination Administered merely to admit a boarding stay.

## Oversight

Use Boarding Dashboard, Boarding Report and Kennel Availability Report with the correct branch and date filters. Capacity totals must be reconciled to booking and stay records before accepting additional animals.

## Practice Exercise

Create a synthetic boarding booking, confirm a kennel, record one care activity and demonstrate the clinical escalation path for an animal that becomes unwell.
