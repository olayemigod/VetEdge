## Capture Safety Notes

- Use only demo or training records.
- Do not submit invoices, payments, stock entries, vaccinations, or discharges while capturing screenshots.
- Hide patient-owner personal information unless the data is fully synthetic.
- After capture, update the relevant row status from `Pending` to `Captured`.
