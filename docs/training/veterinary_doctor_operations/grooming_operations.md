# Grooming Operations

## Module Purpose

Progress grooming appointments and sessions safely, record service evidence and escalate medical concerns into the clinical workflow.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

1. Open Veterinary Home, the grooming workspace or assigned Grooming Appointment.
2. Confirm patient, owner, branch, service, appointment time and groomer assignment.
3. Review alerts and the medical-safety information available to the role.
4. Begin the Grooming Session only when the patient and service are correct.
5. Record service progress, notes and completion using the permitted actions.
6. If pain, wound, infection, parasites, breathing difficulty, severe anxiety, aggression or another clinical concern is found, stop the unsafe action and notify Front Desk and a practitioner.
7. Use the authorised consultation or Hospitalisation workflow for clinical care; do not document a diagnosis in the grooming record.
8. Complete the service record and send billing or stock exceptions to the authorised team.

## Boundaries

Grooming is a non-clinical service workflow. It does not replace consultation, Vital Signs, vaccination, laboratory or Hospitalisation records. A medical concern discovered during grooming must be handed into the clinical system.

## Practice Exercise

Progress a synthetic grooming session, identify one medical stop condition, and prepare a complete handoff to Front Desk and the practitioner.
