# Veterinary Nursing Operations

## Module Purpose

Support practitioners with structured observations, inpatient care and assigned clinical activities while preserving practitioner authority and patient identity.

## Daily Procedure

1. Open Veterinary Home and confirm branch, date and assigned work.
2. Verify patient identity, Primary Owner, consultation or Hospitalisation Episode before documentation.
3. Record observations in Veterinary Vital Signs when the workflow permits; do not hide structured observations inside free-text notes.
4. Record assigned inpatient care through the secured Hospitalisation Episode actions, such as nursing notes, feeding, fluid therapy, wound care or other authorised activities.
5. Escalate abnormal vitals, deterioration, medication concerns or discharge blockers to the attending practitioner immediately.
6. Open notifications and complete only work genuinely finished.
7. Review nurse-visible reports using the correct branch and date scope.

## Medical History Effect

Saved structured Vital Signs contribute to Medical History and trends. Inpatient activities contribute to the Hospitalisation timeline. Linked lab, vaccination and vital records are not duplicated merely by describing them again in a hospital note.

## Boundaries

- Do not select a diagnosis or change a treatment plan without practitioner authority.
- Do not administer medication, vaccination or therapy outside the clinic's authorised care instruction.
- Do not complete a Lab Order or vaccination merely to clear a queue.
- Do not post stock or billing adjustments outside the authorised workflow.

## Practice Exercise

Record trainer-approved synthetic vitals and one inpatient nursing activity, then verify the Medical History and Hospitalisation timeline without duplicating the observation in free text.
